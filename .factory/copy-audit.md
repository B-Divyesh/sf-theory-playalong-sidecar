# Landing copy audit

Audit method: visible landing-page sentences were split at sentence-ending
punctuation. Button and control labels are listed separately. A word is a
space-separated token. No sentence exceeds 22 words. No text contains a banned
plain-words term.

## Actions and short labels

`Try it with sample data`, `Choose an audio file`, `Connect MIDI`, `Clear
history`, `Export CSV`, `Export JSON`, and `Import JSON` all use a verb and name
their result. Headings describe their section without surrounding text.
